# Enhanced Cookie Clicker Game

A feature-rich Cookie Clicker game implemented with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

- Click the cookie to earn cookies
- Purchase various buildings and upgrades:
  - Automatic buildings (Cursors, Grandmas, Farms, Mines, Factories)
  - Click efficiency upgrades (Reinforced Fingers, Golden Clicker, Super Clicker)
  - Building-specific boosts (Steel-plated Rollers, Grandma's Secret Recipe)
  - Global multipliers (Double Cookies)
- Achievement system with notifications and unlockable rewards
- Statistics tracking for gameplay metrics
- Save system with auto-save functionality
- Sound effects for enhanced gameplay experience
- Customizable settings (sound, animations, auto-save)
- Visual feedback and animations
- Responsive design for all devices

## How to Play

1. Click the cookie to earn cookies
2. Open the shop by clicking the "Shop" button
3. Purchase buildings and upgrades when you have enough cookies
4. Buildings will automatically generate cookies over time
5. Upgrades will enhance your clicking power or building efficiency
6. Unlock achievements as you progress
7. Check your statistics to track your progress

## Game Mechanics

- Each click initially earns 1 cookie
- Buildings automatically generate cookies:
  - Cursors: 0.1 cookies per second
  - Grandmas: 1 cookie per second
  - Farms: 5 cookies per second
  - Mines: 10 cookies per second
  - Factories: 40 cookies per second
- Upgrades enhance your cookie production:
  - Click efficiency upgrades increase cookies per click
  - Building-specific upgrades multiply production of specific buildings
  - Global multipliers affect all cookie production
- Building prices increase with each purchase following a formula
- Some upgrades require other upgrades or building counts to unlock
- Achievements unlock as you reach various milestones

## Settings

- Sound effects: Toggle game sounds on/off
- Animations: Toggle visual effects on/off
- Auto-save: Enable automatic game saving
- Manual save/load/reset options available

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser
